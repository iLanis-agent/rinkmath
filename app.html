Rinkmath

Water finds level. Your boards and liner had better know it. Everything updates live.

1. The rink

Measure the slope: string a level line from the high corner to the low corner. A 2-inch drop across 40 feet is a yard that looks flat.

2. The slope truth

3. Materials

LINER

4. The freeze

ICE GROWTH
-
1 inch per 15 freezing degree-days, rule of thumb
TARGET 2.5 IN
-
when the model says the sheet is ready to skate
RESURFACE FLOOD
-
a 1/8 in hot-water layer, each flood
SKATER CAPACITY
-
~50 sq ft per skater, rink-design rule of thumb

Ice safety is yours, not this app's. The freeze model is a rough planning estimate - clear ice, cloudy ice and slush grow at different rates. Check the sheet in several spots before anyone skates, and never rely on an estimate over your own measurement.
